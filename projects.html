Project Practice Year

Lead Front-End Web Developer for OSC

Open Source Climbing (OSC) is a free training resource app built for college climbing teams. I designed and built a complete visual asset library for the platform — including a color palette, logo, custom emoji set, buttons, and input components — along with a site-wide dark mode toggle. Working closely with the back-end team, I maintained clear, consistent communication to meet deadlines and project goals. Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with hands-on experience picking up React along the way. The project is ongoing and live at opensourceclimbing.com.

Instruction at Studio Two Three

At Studio Two Three, I teach Letterpress instruction covering type nomenclature, composing type, press operation, and press cleaning and setup. The studio's traditional print shop houses several vintage presses donated by museums and private collectors, including the Showcard proof press, the Nolan proof press, and the Challenge 15KA proof press. Sessions cover the fundamentals of printing with both metal and wood type, along with a guided tour of the studio's extensive, century-old type collection that I've personally organized, cleaned, and catalogued. Students leave with their own prints, dried on a convection dryer. These classes typically run in groups of 3–5, though I also teach one-on-one sessions.

I also lead a more approachable beginner class exploring the art of Letterpress using flat LEGO pieces: students compose their own artworks while I demonstrate the studio's various presses, offering a low-pressure introduction to analog printing. These workshops usually run in groups of 5 or more.

My Cyanotype instruction explores printing with natural materials, using photographic images from film or transparencies. I teach both natural UV sunlight exposure and the studio's indoor exposure unit for cloudy days, in classes of up to 15 students. Cyanotype printing is one of my creative passions, and I'm drawn to working with natural materials within artistic practice. I've recently begun experimenting with chlorotype printing and look forward to exploring it further.
